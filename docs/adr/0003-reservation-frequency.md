# ADR-0003: Optimizing for reservation frequency

**Status:** accepted · **Phase:** architecture (pricing sub-decision in the infra/UI phase)

## Context
"Optimize for reservation frequency" can mean *more bookings per conversation* or *more
bookings per second without errors*.

## Options
1. **Both** *(recommended)*
2. Conversion only
3. Write throughput only

## Decision
Both.

**Conversion:**
- **Booking Rescue** (net-new skill) turns blackouts, over-limit blocks, capacity misses and
  sold-out dates into bookable alternatives.
- Proposals inside the rules are held immediately (72 h); flagged ones arrive with options
  sales can confirm today.
- One-tap 15-minute room holds from chat, and a checkout dialog with no page change.
- Cancellation offers "change dates instead" where the rate allows.
- Budget-match pricing: when the requested discount still misses the client's stated
  budget, also offer the smallest whole-percent discount that meets it, only within
  auto-approve authority. INQ-2001: 10% → $197.10 vs a $195 budget; 11% → $194.91.
  Shown as an option, never substituted. Flag: `SOLSTICE_BUDGET_MATCH`.
- Funnel instrumentation (sessions → availability → holds → bookings) on `/staff/metrics`.

**Safety:**
- Room-night inventory rows are locked in date order (no deadlocks), plus
  `CHECK (sold + held <= capacity)`.
- Idempotency keys on holds; TTL holds swept every 15 s, and expired holds count until
  swept (conservative).
- Tested: 40 concurrent holds against 15 suites → exactly 15 succeed.

## Consequences
- Every alternative is computed from data, never generated by the model.
- Hold TTLs trade inventory freshness for conversion; 15 min (stays) and 72 h (groups) are config.
